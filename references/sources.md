# Where to look

Read this during step 3 of `SKILL.md`. Search by ISBN-13 when you have it; it avoids wrong editions. All of these
are public pages: no sign-in is needed to search them.

## Arizona State University (ASU)

- **ASU Library.** Search One Search on https://lib.asu.edu by title or ISBN to see whether the library has the
  book, as an ebook or in print. Switching the search dropdown to the ASU Library Catalog lets you search by ISBN,
  exact title (in quotes) or author.
  - Course reserves: instructors can put books on reserve for their class. Look for the course's reserve listing
    and note the loan length (often a few hours, in the library).
  - Library loans can be recalled by another patron, which can shorten the loan:
    https://lib.asu.edu/access/recall
  - Textbooks for ASU classes cannot be requested through Interlibrary Loan, so do not suggest it.
- **ASU bookstore (Sun Devil Campus Stores).** It is not part of the library.
  https://www.bkstr.com/arizonastatestore
  It can be searched by course, section or ISBN and offers new, used and rental where available. Students also
  reach their own list through My ASU (My Classes, then Books), which needs their ASU sign-in, so prefer asking the
  student for a screenshot of that list over signing in.
- Ask a Librarian, for anything unclear: https://askalibrarian.asu.edu/

## Other schools

Search the web for "<school> library course reserves" and "<school> bookstore textbook lookup". Say which school
the results are for, since campus systems differ.

## Free and open textbooks

Suggest one only when it is the book the course uses, or the instructor says an open alternative is fine.

- OpenStax: https://openstax.org
- LibreTexts: https://libretexts.org
- Open Textbook Library: https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks

## Used, rental and digital

Search the ISBN on well-known sellers and price comparison sites (for example BookFinder, AbeBooks, Amazon,
Chegg, eBay) and on the publisher's or VitalSource's page for digital editions. Record the price the student would
actually pay, including the rental term. Skip marketplace listings that look like unauthorized copies (an "instant
PDF" for a few dollars, "international edition" PDFs sent by email).

## Access codes

Online homework codes (MyLab, Mastering, Connect, WebAssign, ALEKS and similar) are tied to one student and one
course, so used and rented books almost never include a working one. Check whether the publisher sells the code
alone, often with the ebook included, and compare that with the bookstore bundle.
